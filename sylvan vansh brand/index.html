Our Story

Two Childhood Friends.
One Unfinished Dream.

We started with an idea, a friendship, and the courage to build something of our own.

Vansh Gautam and Abhay Chaudhary have been childhood friends. Since childhood, they've shared a dream of building something of their own — instead of waiting for opportunities, they decided to create them. Their friendship is the foundation of Sylvan Studio.

They started with a simple vision: make high-quality creative services accessible at affordable prices. One place where you can get a website, logo & branding, custom posters, photo editing, video editing, and digital solutions — without contacting multiple people.

They are young, but serious about learning, improving, delivering quality, and building a brand that grows with every project.
